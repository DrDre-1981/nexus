Cross-project questions

Only questions that span more than one workspace, or have no home in an existing app. Budget questions stay in the budget app's Unresolved money panel. Pantry questions stay in Pantry Ledger.

Your device data

Checklists, review dates, and questions live in this browser only. The hosted copy and a local file copy keep separate records — move them with these buttons.

What this page does not do. It does not connect to Gemini Notebook, ChatGPT, or Claude. It cannot see what sources a notebook holds, whether they are current, or what changed. Every checklist tick is something you did by hand and told it about. Treat the dates as the only evidence.

Adding or changing a link. Open nexus.html in the repo, find the WORKSPACES block at the top of the script, and edit the url value. Dashed chips are links with no URL set yet.
